## The problem

Tax and compliance requirements differ sharply between visitors. A salaried employee may want to understand which ITR form applies to them; a freelancer may need to compare business-income filing and GST requirements; a startup founder may need incorporation support; and an existing company may be looking for TDS, ROC or annual compliance assistance.

A generic financial-services homepage cannot answer all of these questions without becoming overwhelming. Visitors need to identify their situation, reach the appropriate service, understand the documents or expert assistance involved, and make an informed enquiry. Because the work involves highly sensitive financial and identity information, the website also needs accurate privacy and security explanations rather than decorative trust badges.

The saved project designs focus on **reducing service-discovery friction while conveying the breadth of Tax2Rest's professional assistance**. This is an evidence-based description of the business problem and design direction, **not** a documented audit of a previous live website or a measured usability problem.

## How the website presents the service experience

The points below reflect Tax2Rest's publicly visible site structure and saved design artwork. They are not a reconstruction of an internal delivery timeline, and a visual mockup does not prove that the related backend workflow is deployed.

1. **Lead with a clear service proposition.** The homepage introduces income tax, GST, TDS and company compliance rather than assuming visitors already know which service they need. The published hero uses the message **“File Taxes. Grow Your Business. Stay Compliant.”**
2. **Create separate service families.** The current site and saved service dashboards divide income tax, GST, TDS/TCS, business registration and other compliance categories into recognisable groups.
3. **Make filing options discoverable.** The [Services](https://tax2rest.com/services/) page presents category selectors and a service search field. Income-tax options are broken down by salary, business, capital gains, NRI, HUF and special filing situations.
4. **Explain the assisted-filing process.** The published homepage describes a five-stage journey: choose a service, submit documents, expert review, filing/approval and completion. These are published process descriptions, not confirmation of a tested end-to-end tax filing integration.
5. **Provide a route to expert support.** Calls to action such as *File My Taxes Now*, *Start Filing* and *Talk to a Tax Expert* guide visitors towards initiating their specific request.
6. **Explain different customer needs.** Saved layouts give salaried taxpayers, freelancers, startups and business owners their own service-context modules, helping users see that the platform handles more than individual ITR filing.
7. **Support resource-led discovery.** The public site includes tax guides, compliance checklists, FAQs and other educational materials. Their current tax-year accuracy, file validity and download delivery require separate checks.
8. **Build a professional visual language.** The saved designs use navy, white and green, restrained financial illustrations, category cards and modular sections to convey service clarity rather than a generic corporate landing page.
9. **Offer visible account entry points.** Public [Login](https://tax2rest.com/login) and [Register](https://tax2rest.com/register) pages exist. Their presence **does not prove** secure document encryption, filing status synchronisation, a functioning tax dashboard or completed payment/filing workflows.
10. **Give enquiries their own page.** The [Contact Us](https://tax2rest.com/contact-us/) page publishes telephone, email, WhatsApp, an office address and a service-oriented enquiry form.

## Quality, security and compliance review criteria

These are **recommended checks**, not statements that Tax2Rest passed formal testing, a security audit or professional accreditation.

- **[Core Web Vitals](https://web.dev/articles/vitals):** review homepage and service-page image loading, responsiveness, interaction delays and layout stability.
- **[WCAG 2.2 Level AA](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/):** test category selectors, search fields, file-upload controls, forms, status/error messages, focus behaviour and readable contrast.
- **[Google Search Essentials](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials):** review service-specific content, crawlable page hierarchy, unique titles and accurate tax-year information.
- **[Schema.org](https://schema.org/):** consider appropriate Organisation, Service and BreadcrumbList markup supported by actual business records.
- **Tax-law accuracy:** verify filing forms, eligibility, deadlines, rates and assessment years against current official Income Tax, GST and MCA guidance. Do not treat examples in older artwork as current advice.
- **Professional status:** verify the credentials and authorisations of individual tax professionals. Government-body logos on a site do not by themselves demonstrate a government partnership or endorsement.
- **Sensitive document handling:** test HTTPS, access controls, authentication, storage encryption, retention/deletion, upload validation, secure backups and breach procedures before claiming “bank-level security” or end-to-end confidentiality.
- **Price transparency:** reconcile service fees across homepage sections, the services catalogue, filing forms and published terms; make inclusions, taxes and refunds clear.
- **Privacy and consent:** ensure appropriate handling of PAN, Aadhaar, financial records, credentials, taxpayer data and contact-form information under applicable Indian legal requirements.
- **Claims and social proof:** independently validate client counts, returns processed, turnaround, ratings, CA headcount and testimonials before presenting them as guaranteed or audited outcomes.
