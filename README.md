## The problem

Tax and compliance requirements differ sharply between visitors. A salaried employee may want to understand which ITR form applies; a freelancer may need to compare business-income filing and GST requirements; a startup founder may need incorporation support; and an existing company may be looking for TDS, ROC or annual compliance assistance.

A generic financial-services homepage cannot answer all of these questions without becoming overwhelming. Visitors need to identify their situation, reach the appropriate service, understand the documents or expert assistance involved, and make an informed enquiry. Because the work involves highly sensitive financial and identity information, the website also needs accurate privacy and security explanations rather than decorative trust badges.

The supplied website media supports a visual comparison between an earlier and redesigned presentation focused on reducing service-discovery friction. It does not by itself prove improved conversions, successful filing completion or stronger business performance.

## How the website presents the service experience

The points below reflect Tax2Rest's publicly visible site structure and supplied project media. They are not a reconstruction of an internal delivery timeline, and a visual mockup does not prove that the related backend workflow is deployed.

1. **Lead with a clear service proposition.** The homepage introduces income tax, GST, TDS and company compliance rather than assuming visitors already know which service they need.
2. **Create separate service families.** Income tax, GST, TDS/TCS, business registration and other compliance categories are presented as recognisable groups.
3. **Make filing options discoverable.** The [Services](https://tax2rest.com/services/) page presents category selectors and service search, with income-tax options divided by salary, business, capital gains, NRI, HUF and special filing situations.
4. **Explain the assisted-filing process.** Public content describes a staged journey from service selection and document submission through expert review and filing. This does not confirm a tested end-to-end government integration.
5. **Provide a route to expert support.** Calls to action guide users toward a relevant tax or compliance enquiry instead of making them interpret every service unaided.
6. **Explain different customer needs.** Salaried taxpayers, freelancers, startups and business owners can be presented through relevant service contexts rather than one undifferentiated catalogue.
7. **Support resource-led discovery.** Tax guides, compliance checklists and FAQs can support decision-making, but current tax-year accuracy should be checked before publication.
8. **Build a professional visual language.** Navy, white and green, restrained financial illustrations, category cards and modular sections create a clearer professional-service identity.
9. **Offer visible account entry points.** Public login and registration interfaces exist, but these do **not** prove secure document encryption, filing-status synchronisation, a functioning tax dashboard or completed payment/filing workflows.
10. **Give enquiries their own route.** The contact experience provides direct assistance options and a service-oriented enquiry path.

## Quality, security and compliance review criteria

These are **recommended checks**, not statements that Tax2Rest passed formal testing, a security audit or professional accreditation.

- **[Core Web Vitals](https://web.dev/articles/vitals):** review homepage and service-page image loading, responsiveness, interaction delays and layout stability.
- **[WCAG 2.2 Level AA](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/):** test category selectors, search fields, file-upload controls, forms, status/error messages, focus behaviour and readable contrast.
- **[Google Search Essentials](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials):** review service-specific content, crawlable page hierarchy, unique titles and accurate tax-year information.
- **[Schema.org](https://schema.org/):** consider appropriate Organisation, Service and BreadcrumbList markup supported by actual business records.
- **Tax-law accuracy:** verify filing forms, eligibility, deadlines, rates and assessment years against current official Income Tax, GST and MCA guidance. Do not treat examples in older artwork as current advice.
- **Professional status:** verify the credentials and authorisations of individual tax professionals. Government-body logos on a site do not by themselves demonstrate a government partnership or endorsement.
- **Sensitive document handling:** test HTTPS, access controls, authentication, storage encryption, retention/deletion, upload validation, secure backups and breach procedures before claiming “bank-level security” or end-to-end confidentiality.
- **Price transparency:** reconcile service fees across homepage sections, the services catalogue, filing forms and published terms; make inclusions, taxes and refunds clear.
- **Privacy and consent:** ensure appropriate handling of PAN, Aadhaar, financial records, credentials, taxpayer data and contact-form information under applicable Indian legal requirements.
- **Claims and social proof:** independently validate client counts, returns processed, turnaround, ratings, CA headcount and testimonials before presenting them as guaranteed or audited outcomes.
